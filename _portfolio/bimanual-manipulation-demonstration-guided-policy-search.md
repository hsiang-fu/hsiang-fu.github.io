## Overview

This project converts teleoperated bimanual demonstrations into parameterized 3-D wrist trajectories that can be searched and refined. It combines demonstration-guided initialization with automated policy optimization in simulation.

## Research Focus

- Teleoperated bimanual demonstrations
- Parameterized 3-D wrist trajectories
- LLM-generated Dynamic Movement Primitive proposals
- Iterative policy optimization in simulation

**Timeline:** August 2025 - Present
