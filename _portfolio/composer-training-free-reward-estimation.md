## Overview

ComposeR develops dense, interpretable progress rewards directly from robot trajectory video without task-specific reward-model training. The method combines vision-language model token probabilities with multi-view evidence, verification, and temporal checks.

## Current Results

The system achieved **92.35% task-outcome prediction accuracy** in current evaluations.

## Research Focus

- Training-free robot reward estimation
- Vision-language model evidence
- Multi-view trajectory verification
- Temporally consistent progress assessment
