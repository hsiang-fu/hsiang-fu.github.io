## Overview

UR5 Golf studies in-context policy search through a physical robot putting task. The system connects real-world rollouts, camera feedback, and trajectory optimization in a repeatable experimental pipeline.

## Research Focus

- Physical rollout and camera-feedback loops
- Dynamic Movement Primitive fitting
- Global search over trajectory parameters
- In-context robot policy improvement
