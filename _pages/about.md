      <h2 id="about-heading">Learning systems for the real world.</h2>
      <p>My work connects machine learning, human-centered design, and embodied intelligence.</p>
    </header>

    <div class="signal-about__grid">
      <div class="signal-about__copy">
        <p>My research explores how learning-based methods can help robots perceive, reason, adapt, and collaborate in complex environments. I am especially interested in systems that learn from demonstrations, feedback, multimodal context, and changing human goals.</p>
        <p>Before ASU, I completed a B.S. in Informatics with a specialization in Human-Centered Data Science at the University of Texas at Austin. Work across reinforcement learning, multimodal systems, human-AI interaction, and applied machine learning shaped how I think about useful intelligent systems.</p>

    <div class="signal-capabilities">
      <article><span>SYS.01</span><h3>In-Context Learning</h3><p>Adapting behavior from instructions, examples, and task context without retraining from scratch.</p><ul><li>Prompted adaptation</li><li>Demonstrations</li><li>Generalization</li></ul></article>
      <article><span>SYS.02</span><h3>Reward Models</h3><p>Learning useful feedback signals from preferences, multimodal observations, and task outcomes.</p><ul><li>Preference learning</li><li>Evaluation</li><li>Alignment</li></ul></article>
      <article><span>SYS.03</span><h3>Agentic AI</h3><p>Connecting perception, planning, memory, and action in systems that can pursue longer-horizon goals.</p><ul><li>Planning</li><li>Tool use</li><li>Memory</li></ul></article>
      <article><span>SYS.04</span><h3>Reinforcement Learning</h3><p>Improving decisions through interaction while staying robust to noisy and shifting constraints.</p><ul><li>Policy learning</li><li>Control</li><li>Robustness</li></ul></article>
      <article><span>SYS.05</span><h3>Human-Robot Collaboration</h3><p>Keeping people, intent, and uncertainty visible inside the robot's learning and decision loop.</p><ul><li>Human feedback</li><li>Shared autonomy</li><li>Interaction</li></ul></article>
